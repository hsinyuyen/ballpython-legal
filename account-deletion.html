Account & Data Deletion

App: Ball Python King · Developer: EK Reptiles

This page explains how to request deletion of your account and associated data for the game Ball Python King.

How to request deletion

  1. Send an email to edwardhsinbusiness@gmail.com from the email address associated with your account (the Apple or Google account you signed in with).
  2. Use the subject line Delete my account.
  3. We will verify the request and delete your account and associated data within 30 days, and reply to confirm.

If you played only as a guest (never signed in), no account exists on our servers — simply uninstalling the app removes your local data.

Data that is deleted

  • Account identifier (Apple/Google user id)
  • Email address and display name
  • Profile photo (avatar), if provided
  • Cloud-saved game progress (snakes, currency, collection, rewards)

Data that may be retained

Purchase/transaction records may be retained for the period required by law (e.g. tax and accounting obligations), in de-identified form where possible. Such records are not used to re-identify you after deletion.
